     Each step's screenshot is web/static/img/guides/kroger-N.png - replace
     the file to update it. */}}
{{template "partials/modal_open.html" dict "ID" "kroger-guide" "Title" "Kroger API - Setup guide" "Large" true}}

    
    
    
        
    1Sign in to Kroger Developers
    
        
    Go to developer.kroger.com and sign in or create a free account. Open My Apps and click Create App.
    
        
        
    
    

    
    
        
    2Describe the app and pick Production
    
        
    Enter any name and description (e.g. GoEat, "meal planning with Kroger prices"). Set Environment to Production before choosing API products. App homepage, redirect URI, terms and privacy links can stay blank.
    
        
        
    
    

    
    
        
    3Select the API products
    
        
    Tick only Products (Public) (prices) and Locations (Public) (store lookup for Test connection). Leave Cart and Profile unticked - they need a customer login GoEat doesn't use. Click Create App.
    
        
    "Products do not match the chosen environment" error? The form kept a stale product list. Open a private/incognito window, create a new app, and pick Production first.
    
        
        
    
    

    
    
        
    4Copy the Client ID and secret
    
        
    Open the new app. Paste its Client ID and Client Secret into the matching fields on this card. Watch for trailing spaces when pasting.
    
        
        
    
    

    
    
        
    5Find your store's Location ID
    
        
    On kroger.com (or Ralphs, Fred Meyer, King Soopers, etc.) find your store and open its store details page. The URL ends in two number groups - join them:
    
        
    .../stores/details/014/00338 → 01400338
    
        
        
    
    

    
    
        
    6Test and restart
    
        
    Click Test connection on this card. You should see "token OK", your store's name and address, and a sample price. Then restart GoEat (make docker-restart) so plan pricing picks up the new credentials.

    {{icon "alert"}} Danger zone
    
    
    
        These permanently delete data for this household. There is no undo and no archive -
        unlike regenerating a plan, nothing here is kept for history. Type carefully.
    
    

    
    
        
    
            Recipe catalog
            
    Deletes every saved recipe (imported or hand-entered) under /recipes.
    
        
    
        
    
            {{$.CSRFField}}
            
        
    
    
    

    
    
        
    
            Pantry
            
    Deletes every pantry item and stock level.
    
        
    
        
    
            {{$.CSRFField}}
            
        
    
    
    

    
    
        
    
            Shopping lists
            
    Clears every generated shopping-list line (checked items, prices) across all plans - the plans and meals themselves are untouched.
    
        
    
        
    
            {{$.CSRFField}}
            
        
    
    
    

    
    
        
    
            Meal plans
            
    Deletes every weekly plan - past and present - along with their meals, recipes, ingredients, and shopping lists. Unlike a regenerate, this does not keep anything in Plan History.
    
        
    
        
    
            {{$.CSRFField}}
            
        
    
    
    

    
    
        
    
            Everything
            
    Runs every wipe above at once: recipes, pantry, shopping lists, and meal plans (with all history). This is the whole household's data, gone.
    
        
    
        
    
            {{$.CSRFField}}
