{{icon "help"}} What can I import?
{{icon "book"}}Recipe websites

Most recipe sites and food blogs - anything that publishes standard recipe data (Schema.org), which covers the big recipe sites and blogs using recipe plugins like WP Recipe Maker or Tasty Recipes. Paste the page of one recipe, not a search or category page. Pages behind a paywall or login can't be read.

{{icon "video"}}Cooking videos {{if and $v.HasLLM (eq $v.Capability "full")}}Ready {{else if and $v.HasLLM (eq $v.Capability "caption")}}Captions only {{else}}Not set up{{end}}
  • TikTok - tiktok.com/@user/video/… and vm.tiktok.com/… share links
  • Instagram - Reels and video posts (instagram.com/reel/…). Some posts need a login and can't be read.
  • YouTube - Shorts and regular videos up to 15 minutes

Go Eat reads the caption, the creator's own and pinned comments (YouTube and Instagram), and listens to what's said, then the AI writes it up as a recipe. Amounts the video never states are estimated and the recipe is tagged estimated-amounts - check them before you cook. Text that only appears on screen isn't read yet, and if the creator says the recipe is "at the link in bio", import that page instead.
