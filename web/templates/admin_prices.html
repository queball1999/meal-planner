Getting started

  1. Pick a store from the list below.
  2. Enter the ingredient name as it appears in a recipe (e.g. "chicken breast").
  3. Enter the price per pack and pack size (e.g. $8.99 for a 2 lb pack → size 2, unit lb).
  4. Save. This price will be used whenever the pricing chain can't find a live price for that store.
